# slang

## 1. Core Vocabulary & Idiomatic Expressions

- **Cool** /kuːl/ *(adjective)* — Great, fashionable, or acceptable.
  - *Example:* That new blue jacket is really cool.
- **Hang out** /hæŋ aʊt/ *(phrasal verb)* — To spend time relaxing with people.
  - *Example:* We can hang out at the park today.
- **Chill** /tʃɪl/ *(verb / adjective)* — To relax; calm and easygoing.
  - *Example:* I stayed home because I wanted to chill.
- **No worries** /noʊ ˈwɜːriz/ *(phrase)* — It is not a problem; you are welcome.
  - *Example:* Thanks for the help! — No worries, anytime.
- **Stuff** /stʌf/ *(noun)* — General things, items, or activities.
  - *Example:* I need to buy some stuff for dinner.
- **Guy / Guys** /ɡaɪz/ *(noun)* — A man; (plural) a group of people.
  - *Example:* Those guys are going to play football together.

## 2. Grammar & Sentence Pattern Focus

### Pattern 1: Informal Plans with *be going to*
- **Structure:** Subject + *be (am/is/are)* + going to + Base Verb
- **Explanation:** Used to express clear intentions and casual future plans.
- **Examples:**
  1. I am going to chill on the sofa tonight.
  2. We are going to hang out after school.
  3. Are you going to buy that cool shirt?

### Pattern 2: Giving Reasons with *because*
- **Structure:** Main Clause + *because* + Reason Clause
- **Explanation:** Connects a situation with a simple, direct cause.
- **Examples:**
  1. He stayed home because he was very tired.
  2. We met up because we had free time.

## 3. Reading Passage & Dialogue

**Leo:** Hey Sam! What are you going to do this weekend?  
**Sam:** Not much. I am going to chill at home on Saturday.  
**Leo:** Do you want to hang out in the city center?  
**Sam:** That sounds cool. What time can we meet up?  
**Leo:** We can meet at two o'clock because I have lunch first.  
**Sam:** Perfect. I need to buy some stuff for my computer.  
**Leo:** Cool, we can visit the tech shop near the station.  
**Sam:** Great! My brother and his friends might join us too.  
**Leo:** Nice, those guys are always very funny and friendly.  
**Sam:** Thanks for inviting me, Leo. I really needed a break.  
**Leo:** No worries, man. It is going to be a fun day!
