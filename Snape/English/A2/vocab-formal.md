# vocab-formal

## 1. Core Vocabulary & Idiomatic Expressions

- **assist** /əˈsɪst/ (*verb*) – to help someone with a task or problem.
  - *Example*: The hotel staff could assist you with your heavy luggage.
- **receive** /rɪˈsiːv/ (*verb*) – to get something that someone sends to you.
  - *Example*: I received a polite email from the manager yesterday morning.
- **require** /rɪˈkwaɪər/ (*verb*) – to need something for a specific rule or purpose.
  - *Example*: Hotel guests require a key card to enter the building.
- **apologize** /əˈpɒl.ə.dʒaɪz/ (*verb*) – to tell someone that you are sorry.
  - *Example*: The clerk apologized because the morning shuttle was very late.
- **inform** /ɪnˈfɔːm/ (*verb*) – to give official facts or details to someone.
  - *Example*: We will inform the reception desk before we leave tomorrow.
- **would like** /wʊd laɪk/ (*phrase*) – a polite phrase used to say "want".
  - *Example*: I would like to reserve a quiet room for tonight.

## 2. Grammar & Sentence Pattern Focus

### Pattern 1: Polite Requests with *Could you please + base verb*
- **Structure**: `Could you please + [Base Verb] + [Object]?`
- **Explanation**: Use this pattern in formal situations to ask for help or action politely instead of using "Can you" or "Give me".
- **Examples**:
  - *Could you please assist me with this train schedule today?*
  - *Could you please inform the driver about our late arrival?*
  - *Could you please send the bill to my office email?*

### Pattern 2: Stating Formal Plans with *be going to + formal verb*
- **Structure**: `Subject + am/is/are going to + [Base Verb] + [Object]`
- **Explanation**: Use this structure to announce planned, official actions clearly and politely.
- **Examples**:
  - *The hotel manager is going to contact all guests today.*
  - *We are going to apologize for the delay right now.*
  - *I am going to receive my travel tickets this afternoon.*

## 3. Reading Passage & Dialogue

**At the Grand Hotel Reception**

*Guest*: Good afternoon. I would like to check in for my reservation, please.

*Receptionist*: Good afternoon, sir. Welcome to our hotel. Could you please show me your passport?

*Guest*: Here is my passport. I received your confirmation email yesterday afternoon.

*Receptionist*: Thank you. I see your details now. You require a quiet room for two nights.

*Guest*: That is correct, but could you assist me with the city tour bus?

*Receptionist*: Certainly. We are going to arrange a guided tour tomorrow morning at nine.

*Guest*: That is wonderful because I really want to visit the national museum.

*Receptionist*: I apologize for the short wait, but your room is ready on the third floor.

*Guest*: Thank you very much. I will inform my family about our comfortable room.

*Receptionist*: You are welcome. Please contact the front desk if you need anything else today.
