# cheatsheet

## 1. Core Vocabulary & Idiomatic Expressions

- **Cheatsheet** (`/ˈtʃiːt.ʃiːt/`, noun): A small page with quick notes and useful tips.
  - *Example*: I made a cheatsheet because it helps me remember grammar rules.
- **Summary** (`/ˈsʌm.ər.i/`, noun): A short text that gives the main ideas.
  - *Example*: She wrote a quick summary of the English lesson yesterday.
- **Look up** (`/lʊk ʌp/`, phrasal verb): To find information in a book or online.
  - *Example*: You can look up new words on your phone quickly.
- **Organize** (`/ˈɔː.ɡən.aɪz/`, verb): To arrange things in a clear and neat order.
  - *Example*: He likes to organize his study notes before every class.
- **Helpful** (`/ˈhelp.fəl/`, adjective): Useful and making a job or task easier.
  - *Example*: This simple guide is very helpful for my weekly test.
- **Quickly** (`/ˈkwɪk.li/`, adverb): Fast and without wasting any extra time.
  - *Example*: We can read the short list quickly on the bus.

## 2. Grammar & Sentence Pattern Focus

### Pattern 1: *Going to* + Verb (Future Plans & Intentions)
- **Structure**: Subject + `be` (am / is / are) + `going to` + `base verb`
- **Explanation**: Use this pattern to talk about actions you already decided to do.
- **Examples**:
  1. I am going to make a travel cheatsheet tonight.
  2. We are going to study the vocabulary list together tomorrow.
  3. Is she going to print the summary after dinner?

### Pattern 2: Modal *Can* / *Could* for Ability & Polite Requests
- **Structure**: Subject + `can / could` + `base verb`
- **Explanation**: Use *can* for present ability and *could* for polite requests.
- **Examples**:
  1. You can find key words easily on this small page.
  2. Could you share your grammar notes with me, please?
  3. He could not finish his summary yesterday afternoon.

## 3. Reading Passage & Dialogue

**Title: Preparing the Travel Notes**

**Leo**: What are you writing in your notebook today, Maya?  
**Maya**: I am making a simple travel cheatsheet for our trip.  
**Leo**: That sounds great, but why do you need one?  
**Maya**: It helps me remember important words and emergency numbers quickly.  
**Leo**: That is smart because I often forget basic phrases abroad.  
**Maya**: Look, I organized the train times and hotel address nicely.  
**Leo**: Could you make a copy of this list for me?  
**Maya**: Yes, I am going to print two copies after dinner.  
**Leo**: Perfect, now we can find good food without any stress.  
**Maya**: Exactly, and we can also add key local words tomorrow.  
**Leo**: I am going to pack our bags right now, Maya.  
**Maya**: Great, this little sheet will make our weekend very easy!
