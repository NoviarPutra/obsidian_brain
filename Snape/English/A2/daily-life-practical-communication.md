# Daily Life & Practical Communication

## 1. Core Vocabulary & Idiomatic Expressions

- **Grocery list** (`/ˈɡroʊ.sə.ri lɪst/` - noun)
  *Definition:* A written list of foods and household items to buy.
  *Example:* I always write a grocery list before I go shopping.

- **Catch the bus** (`/kætʃ ðə bʌs/` - verb phrase)
  *Definition:* To arrive on time and board a public bus.
  *Example:* She walked quickly because she wanted to catch the bus.

- **Look for** (`/lʊk fɔːr/` - phrasal verb)
  *Definition:* To try to find a person or a misplaced object.
  *Example:* He is looking for his house keys in the kitchen.

- **Appointment** (`/əˈpɔɪnt.mənt/` - noun)
  *Definition:* An agreed time to meet a professional, such as a doctor.
  *Example:* I have a doctor appointment tomorrow morning at nine o'clock.

- **Neighborhood** (`/ˈneɪ.bər.hʊd/` - noun)
  *Definition:* The local area of a town where people live.
  *Example:* There is a quiet park near my new neighborhood.

- **Make dinner** (`/meɪk ˈdɪn.ər/` - verb phrase)
  *Definition:* To prepare and cook the main evening meal.
  *Example:* We are going to make dinner together this evening.

## 2. Grammar & Sentence Pattern Focus

### Pattern 1: Future Intentions with "be going to"
- **Structure:** `Subject` + `am / is / are` + `going to` + `Base Verb`
- **Explanation:** Use this structure to talk about plans decided before the moment of speaking.
- **Examples:**
  1. I am going to buy fresh vegetables at the store.
  2. They are going to visit their relatives this Saturday afternoon.
  3. She is not going to cook dinner tonight.

### Pattern 2: Compound Past Sentences with Conjunctions ("and", "but", "because")
- **Structure:** `Past Clause` + `and / but / because` + `Past Clause`
- **Explanation:** Connect two past actions or explain reasons for daily events.
- **Examples:**
  1. I went to the post office, but it was closed.
  2. He stayed home yesterday because he felt very tired.
  3. We cleaned the apartment and washed all the dirty dishes.

## 3. Reading Passage & Dialogue

**Leo:** Good morning, Maya. What are your plans for today?  
**Maya:** Good morning, Leo. I am going to do some chores.  
**Leo:** Did you make a grocery list for the supermarket?  
**Maya:** Yes, I wrote one because we need bread and eggs.  
**Leo:** Great. Can you buy some fresh fruit too, please?  
**Maya:** Sure, I can do that after my dentist appointment.  
**Leo:** What time is your appointment in the city center?  
**Maya:** It is at two o'clock near the main station.  
**Leo:** Are you going to catch the bus this afternoon?  
**Maya:** No, I walked yesterday, but today I will cycle instead.  
**Leo:** Perfect. We can make dinner together when you return.  
**Maya:** That sounds wonderful. I am leaving the house now.  
**Leo:** Have a nice day, and see you this evening!

## 4. Comprehension & Usage Check

1. **Information Retrieval:** Why did Maya write a grocery list before leaving?
2. **Context Clues:** How is Maya planning to travel to her two o'clock appointment?
3. **Sentence Completion:** Fill in the blank using *and*, *but*, or *because*:  
   *"Leo asked for fruit, _______ Maya agreed to buy some after her appointment."*
