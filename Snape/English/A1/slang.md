# slang

## 1. Core Vocabulary & Idiomatic Expressions
- **Hey** `/heɪ/` *(interjection)*: Informal word for hello.
  - *Hey, what is your name?*
- **Cool** `/kuːl/` *(adjective)*: Very good, nice, or popular.
  - *Your new blue shoes are cool.*
- **Guy** `/ɡaɪ/` *(noun)*: A man or a boy.
  - *That tall guy is my friend.*
- **Yeah** `/jɛə/` *(adverb)*: Casual word for yes.
  - *Yeah, I like this happy song.*
- **Awesome** `/ˈɔː.səm/` *(adjective)*: Great or very good.
  - *This hot pizza is really awesome.*
- **Chill** `/tʃɪl/` *(verb)*: To relax or rest calmly.
  - *We chill at home every Sunday.*

## 2. Grammar & Sentence Pattern Focus

### Pattern 1: `[Subject] + [to be] + [Slang Adjective]`
- **Structure:** `Subject + am / is / are + cool / awesome`
- **Explanation:** Use the verb *to be* with simple informal adjectives to describe things or people.
- **Examples:**
  - *The new game is cool.*
  - *You are awesome today.*
  - *They are chill people.*

### Pattern 2: `Yeah / Nope + [Subject] + [Verb]`
- **Structure:** `Yeah / Nope, + Subject + Verb phrase`
- **Explanation:** Use *yeah* (yes) or *nope* (no) for natural spoken answers.
- **Examples:**
  - *Yeah, I want some tea.*
  - *Nope, he is not here.*
  - *Yeah, we like this place.*

## 3. Reading Passage & Dialogue

**Alex:** Hey Leo, how are you today?  
**Leo:** Hey Alex! I am very good.  
**Alex:** Look at that guy over there.  
**Leo:** Who is he? Is he friendly?  
**Alex:** Yeah, he is my good friend.  
**Leo:** His green bike is so cool!  
**Alex:** Yeah, it is an awesome bike.  
**Leo:** Do you want some juice now?  
**Alex:** Yeah! We can chill right here.  
**Leo:** Great, this warm park is awesome.  
**Alex:** Let us sit on this bench.  
**Leo:** Cool, I like this quiet spot.  
**Alex:** We have a lot of time.  
**Leo:** Yeah, today is a nice day.
