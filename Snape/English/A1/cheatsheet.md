# cheatsheet

## 1. Core Vocabulary & Idiomatic Expressions

- **paper** `/ˈpeɪ.pər/` (noun) — A thin material for writing.
  - *Example:* I write on this paper.
- **note** `/noʊt/` (noun) — A short written reminder.
  - *Example:* She reads a short note.
- **word** `/wɜːrd/` (noun) — A single unit of language.
  - *Example:* I learn one new word.
- **easy** /ˈiː.zi/ (adjective) — Simple and not hard.
  - *Example:* This English test is easy.
- **help** `/help/` (verb) — To make work easier.
  - *Example:* Good notes help me study.
- **write** `/raɪt/` (verb) — To make words with a pen.
  - *Example:* We write words in class.

## 2. Grammar & Sentence Pattern Focus

### Pattern 1: `Subject + have / has + [noun]`
- **Structure:**
  - `I / You / We / They + have + [noun]`
  - `He / She / It + has + [noun]`
- **Explanation:** Use this pattern to talk about things you carry or own.
- **Examples:**
  - I have a small paper.
  - She has five new words.
  - We have easy study notes.

### Pattern 2: `This is / That is + [noun]`
- **Structure:** `This is / That is + a/an + [noun]`
- **Explanation:** Use *this* for an object near you. Use *that* for an object further away.
- **Examples:**
  - This is my study sheet.
  - That is a helpful list.
  - This is a new word.

## 3. Reading Passage & Dialogue

**Leo:** Hello, Anna! What is that paper?  
**Anna:** This is my small cheatsheet today.  
**Leo:** What words are on your paper?  
**Anna:** I have ten new English words.  
**Leo:** Is this sheet easy to read?  
**Anna:** Yes, the notes are very clear.  
**Leo:** Do you write notes every day?  
**Anna:** Yes, I write notes at home.  
**Leo:** That is a great study habit!  
**Anna:** It helps me learn English fast.  
**Leo:** Can I see your list now?  
**Anna:** Sure! We can study together now.  
**Leo:** Thank you for your kind help.  
**Anna:** You are welcome, my friend.

## 4. Comprehension & Usage Check

1. **Detail Check:** What does Anna have on her paper?
2. **Context Check:** Where does Anna write her study notes?
