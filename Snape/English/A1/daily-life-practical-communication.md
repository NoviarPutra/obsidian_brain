# Daily Life & Practical Communication

## 1. Core Vocabulary & Idiomatic Expressions

* **Morning** (/ˈmɔːr.nɪŋ/ - noun): The early part of the day.
  * *Example*: I drink coffee every morning.
* **Breakfast** (/ˈbrek.fəst/ - noun): The first meal of the day.
  * *Example*: We eat breakfast at eight o'clock.
* **Work** (/wɜːrk/ - verb/noun): An activity or job you do.
  * *Example*: She walks to work every day.
* **Buy** (/baɪ/ - verb): To get something with money.
  * *Example*: They buy fresh bread here today.
* **Friend** (/frend/ - noun): A person you know and like.
  * *Example*: My friend lives in this city.
* **Have a good day** (/hæv ə ɡʊd deɪ/ - phrase): A friendly wish to someone.
  * *Example*: Goodbye! Have a good day, Anna.

## 2. Grammar & Sentence Pattern Focus

### Pattern 1: Present Simple with "to be"
* **Structure**: `Subject + am / is / are + Complement`
* **Explanation**: Use this pattern to state facts, feelings, and locations.
* **Examples**:
  * I am very happy today.
  * The shop is open now.
  * They are at home together.

### Pattern 2: Simple Wh- Questions (Present Simple)
* **Structure**: `Wh-word + do / does + Subject + Verb?`
* **Explanation**: Use this pattern to ask for daily information.
* **Examples**:
  * Where do you buy food?
  * What does he eat daily?
  * Where does she work now?

## 3. Reading Passage & Dialogue

**Tom**: Good morning, Mia! How are you?  
**Mia**: I am good, thank you, Tom!  
**Tom**: Where do you go this morning?  
**Mia**: I go to the small bakery.  
**Tom**: What do you buy there today?  
**Mia**: I buy fresh bread and milk.  
**Tom**: Is the bread very good there?  
**Mia**: Yes, it is warm and fresh.  
**Tom**: I want some coffee now too.  
**Mia**: Come with me to the shop!  
**Tom**: That is great! Let us go.  
**Mia**: We can eat breakfast together now.  
**Tom**: Wonderful! Have a good day, everyone.  
**Mia**: Thank you! Let us walk fast.
