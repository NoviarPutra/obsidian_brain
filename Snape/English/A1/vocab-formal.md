# vocab-formal

## 1. Core Vocabulary & Idiomatic Expressions

- **Good morning** /ɡʊd ˈmɔːnɪŋ/ (phrase) — Polite greeting used before 12:00 PM.
  - *Good morning, Mr. Smith. Welcome back.*
- **Please** /pliːz/ (adverb) — Polite word used when making requests.
  - *Sit down here near the desk, please.*
- **Thank you** /ˈθæŋk juː/ (phrase) — Polite expression used to show gratitude.
  - *Thank you for your kind help today.*
- **Excuse me** /ɪkˈskjuːz miː/ (phrase) — Polite phrase used to get attention.
  - *Excuse me, is this your warm jacket?*
- **Pleased to meet you** /pliːzd tuː miːt juː/ (phrase) — Polite greeting when meeting someone new.
  - *Hello, John. Pleased to meet you today.*
- **You are welcome** /juː ɑːr ˈwelkəm/ (phrase) — Polite response after someone says thank you.
  - *You are welcome. Have a good day.*

## 2. Grammar & Sentence Pattern Focus

### Pattern 1: Offering Polite Help with "May I..."
- **Structure:** `May I + verb + you?`
- **Explanation:** Use "May I" to offer assistance in a respectful way.
- **Examples:**
  - *May I help you with your bag?*
  - *May I take your winter coat, please?*
  - *May I ask your full name, Sir?*

### Pattern 2: Polite Inquiries with "Where is..."
- **Structure:** `Where is + Title / Person / Room?`
- **Explanation:** Use "Where is" to ask for locations politely.
- **Examples:**
  - *Where is Mr. Davis right now, please?*
  - *Excuse me, where is room number four?*
  - *Where is the main office this morning?*

## 3. Reading Passage & Dialogue

**Receptionist:** Good morning, Sir. Welcome to our office.  
**Mr. Davis:** Good morning. My name is Paul Davis.  
**Receptionist:** Pleased to meet you, Mr. Davis.  
**Receptionist:** May I take your jacket, please?  
**Mr. Davis:** Yes, please. Thank you very much.  
**Mr. Davis:** Is Ms. Miller in her office today?  
**Receptionist:** Yes, she is in room four.  
**Mr. Davis:** Excuse me, where is room four?  
**Receptionist:** It is down the hall on left.  
**Receptionist:** Please wait here for two minutes.  
**Mr. Davis:** Thank you for your kind help.  
**Receptionist:** You are welcome. Have a good day.  
**Mr. Davis:** Thank you. Goodbye, have a nice day.
